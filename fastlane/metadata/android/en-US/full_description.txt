Jps is a simple maps app with no ads, no accounts and no trackers. It uses OpenStreetMap maps and open services, so it needs no Google services or API keys.

Features:
• Place search with history and favorites
• My location (GPS), zoom, scale and compass
• Two-finger map rotation
• Five map styles to choose from: OpenStreetMap, OSM France, Humanitarian, CyclOSM and OpenTopoMap
• Exact outline of the searched area (countries, provinces, cities)
• Country and state or province borders, plus colors or flags for each country
• 3D globe with a relief mode (mountains and sunken oceans)
• Directions inside the app: car, motorbike, truck, bike or on foot, with custom speed, estimated time, steps and an option to stay inside the country
• Photos and Wikipedia information in the place card
• Share and copy coordinates
• Four languages: Spanish, English, Mandarin Chinese and French
• Light, dark or system theme
• Metric or imperial units

Privacy: no connection is made until you accept the privacy policy, and every feature that uses a new service asks for its own permission. Routes, searches and photos come from open services such as Nominatim, FOSSGIS OSRM and Wikipedia.

Open source under the MIT license.
